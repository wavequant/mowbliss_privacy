Mowbliss Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Mowbliss ("the game", Android package com.perfectlawn.game) is a single-player lawn-mowing game. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it collects none.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, currency, tool upgrades) and your settings (sound, graphics quality, controls) in the game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

Permissions

The game asks only for Vibrate, so your phone can give haptic feedback when you mow, cut fruit or hit an obstacle. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. Google Play (the store you download from) processes your download and any reviews you write under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game contains no ads, chat, social features or purchases, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example advertising or analytics), this policy will be updated first, the date above will change, and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated to match.

Data deletion

Because nothing is collected or sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the game stored on your phone, uninstall it or choose Settings > Apps > Mowbliss > Storage > Clear data.
